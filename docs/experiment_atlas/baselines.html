LIBERO: π₀ and π₀.₅

Success rates for the released pi0_libero and pi05_libero checkpoints across every LIBERO task, before policy learning.

130 tasks · 25 episodes per task and checkpoint · Saved evidence 2026-10-31

Evaluation protocol

Both checkpoints use seed 100 and reset-state IDs 25–49. π₀ executes up to 20 actions per chunk with a 400-step horizon; π₀.₅ executes up to 10 actions per chunk with a 600-step horizon on LIBERO-10 and 400 steps on the other suites. The LIBERO-10 comparison therefore uses different horizons; its difference does not isolate model quality.

Task IDs are zero-based. Success means simulator task completion. Click any success rate to inspect its original evaluation summary, episode outcomes, and recorded Wilson interval; the neighboring config.json records the protocol.

Which suites were used in training?

LIBERO-Spatial, Object, Goal, and LIBERO-10 are included in the documented LIBERO training data for both pi0_libero and pi05_libero, and in the RBM-1M training data for the general Robometer-4B used in this repository’s reported reward-learning runs. LIBERO-90 is held out from these documented training splits.

“Included” describes suite membership, not verified overlap with these particular evaluation trajectories or reset states. “Held out” describes tasks within the same simulator; broader foundation-model pretraining coverage is not established. Robometer coverage here applies to robometer/Robometer-4B@beef63bc914c5c189329d49c6d712d96d632aa34; separately fine-tuned Robometer variants may use different splits. These baseline success rates evaluate the π policies; Robometer coverage is provided as context for reward-learning comparisons.

Sources: OpenPI checkpoint training configs · OpenPI LIBERO dataset construction · Policy training task list · Robometer paper, Appendix A: LIBERO training split · Local checkpoint provenance
