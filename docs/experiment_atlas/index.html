How to read these experiments

Steps: training physical steps per environment = total physical steps ÷ environment count. Replay chunks, optimizer updates, and evaluation episodes are different units. OGPO report axes remove the documented BC offset.

Seeds: independent training runs, not parallel environments or individual evaluation episodes. Compare matched protocols and budgets before making cross-run claims.

Coverage: this page catalogs checked-in evidence. Historical “running” and “queued” statuses describe the capture date, not processes running today. The archive excludes weights and media; report figures are retained separately.
